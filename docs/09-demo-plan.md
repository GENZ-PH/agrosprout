# 09 — Demo & Pitch Plan

> Limits: **≤7 slides including title and intro**, or a **≤30-second video**. Going over either one makes the project **ineligible**. English only (a video needs English subtitles).
> **Proposed format: 7-slide deck** (decision D6). Data-driven comparisons read better on slides than in 30 seconds of video. A short screen recording can live in the README as a bonus.

## Story spine (one sentence)

> *NASA satellites already know when Mang Rodel's second rice crop will run short of water. AgroSprout puts that knowledge in his pocket, offline, and lets him compare safer rotations against the one he already plants.*

## Slide outline (7 max)

| # | Slide | Content | Must-include items |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Title** | Project name + tagline, team name **Jeoleecation**, challenge "Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data", Iligan City | **Team name, member names, challenge** |
| 2 | **The problem** | Mang Rodel (fictional persona); dry-season shortfall; bare months; soil decline; no connectivity in the field | One strong visual (field photo we own, or a watermarked SI illustration) |
| 3 | **What it does** | Phone screenshots strip: **draw lots on the map → pick crops → dashboard (with ⓘ explainers) → your months vs better months**. No login needed. | Image of the project + user experience |
| 4 | **How NASA data makes it possible** | Data-flow graphic: POWER (rain, ET₀, root-zone wetness), SMAP, SRTM, MODIS + ESA Sentinel-2 → water balance → strategies | **NASA + partner data named** |
| 5 | **Why you can trust it** | Strategy card (why / evidence / benefits / unknowns / check locally) + confidence badge + "supports, doesn't replace your technician" | Shows Validity + farmer-centered design |
| 6 | **Works where farmers are** | No account or login; data stays on the phone; works offline after one online setup; plain-language "What am I looking at?" on every chart | User experience |
| 7 | **What's next** | Cooperatives and technicians, more crops and regions, Cebuano/Filipino, SMS export, validation with local farmers | Future vision |

## Design rules

- Big, readable type; **≤25 words per slide** besides labels.
- Real screenshots from the **deployed** app, not mockups.
- **No NASA logos or mission insignia** in anything SI-generated. Better to avoid official logos completely unless brand guidelines allow it (https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/brand/).
- Any SI-generated image gets a **visible "SI-generated" watermark**.
- No people under 18 in any image or video.
- Host on Google Drive or similar with **"Anyone with the link can view"** and test it in incognito.
