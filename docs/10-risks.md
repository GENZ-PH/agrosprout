## Assumptions to validate (the riskiest first)

1. Rainfed lowland rice–rice–bare is a **typical** rotation for the demo area. *(Ask local participants, mentors, or a technician.)*
2. A dry-season **stress window** actually shows up in the 2001–2025 POWER data for the demo point.
3. Mungbean or another legume after rice is **agronomically realistic** there (drainage, seed, market).
4. Farmers have Android phones that can install an APK (outside the Play Store) and open it once while online.
5. NASA POWER `GWETROOT` is a reasonable proxy for root-zone wetness at the monthly scale (compare with SMAP if available).
