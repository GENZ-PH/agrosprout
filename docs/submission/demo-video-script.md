## Recording setup

- Record the live app at https://agrosprout.pages.dev, **after one reload** (the offline copy updates on the next load), in a phone-sized window (about 390 x 844) or on an Android phone.
- Use **See a sample farm**: two lots in Lanao del Norte, full results, no signal needed.
- Record each shot as a separate clip, 1 to 2 seconds longer than needed; trim in the editor.
- Captions: large, high contrast, one line at a time, on screen at least 1.5 seconds each.
